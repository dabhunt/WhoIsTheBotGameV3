
# Who Is The Bot - Product Requirements Document

## Overview
"Who Is The Bot" is a multiplayer game that presents a unique twist on the Turing test. Players join a chat room where one participant is secretly an AI bot. The objective is to identify which participant is the bot through conversation while avoiding being eliminated.

## Core Concept
- **Players**: 2 human players + 1 AI bot
- **Objective**: Correctly identify which participant is the AI bot
- **Challenge**: The bot attempts to appear human, while humans try to appear more human than the bot

## Key Features
- Real-time multiplayer matchmaking
- In-game text-based chat
- Bot AI that attempts to mimic human conversation patterns
- Time-limited interactions requiring player engagement
- Elimination mechanics based on guessing
- Winner determination based on correct identification

## Target Audience
- Players interested in AI and the Turing test concept
- Casual gamers looking for a quick social deduction game
- People interested in testing their ability to distinguish AI from humans

## Value Proposition
"Who Is The Bot" offers a unique social deduction experience that tests a player's ability to distinguish between human and AI responses. As AI becomes increasingly sophisticated, this game provides both entertainment and insight into how well AI can mimic human communication patterns.
